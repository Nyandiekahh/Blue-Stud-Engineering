## 🎨 Image Style Guidelines

### Quality Standards
- **Resolution:** Minimum 1200px width for service images, 600px for gallery tiles
- **Format:** JPG format (optimized for web)
- **File size:** Keep under 500KB per image for optimal loading speed
- **Aspect ratio:** 3:2 or 4:3 recommended for best display

### Visual Style
- **Professional:** Clean, modern, industrial aesthetic
- **Colors:** Blue tones preferred to match brand (optional)
- **Lighting:** Well-lit, clear visibility of subjects
- **Composition:** Sharp focus, minimal distractions
- **Safety:** Personnel in proper safety gear when applicable
